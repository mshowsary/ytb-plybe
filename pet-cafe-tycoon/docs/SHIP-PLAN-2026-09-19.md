# Pet Café Tycoon — the MediaCube ship plan (2026-09-19)

The owner, 2026-09-19: *"focus completing the game without the SPA, because we need to ship it to
mediacube … revisit everything in the game from day 1 … if something does not make sense re-work
it, and make this a simple, yet enjoyable game for the player to enjoy and keep playing and have
ads earning for us."* This file is the design contract for every batch that follows. It was written
from seven research reports (spa removal, terrace/garden, menu/HUD, economy+ads, onboarding, a
19-day playthrough, a visual-glitch hunt) whose evidence lives in the session scratchpad.

The rules that decide every conflict, in order:

1. **Coherent.** Every cause shows its effect and every object says what it is. Nothing in the world
   that looks interactive may be inert; nothing that matters may be invisible.
2. **Simple.** One place per job, one verb per station, one purpose per purchase. Delete before
   adding. A mechanic that needs a sentence to explain is not designed yet.
3. **Never punishing.** No coin fines, no reputation drains, no soft-locks. Low lost-sales is fine.
   Pressure is never the fun; variety and moments are.
4. **Pets are the hook.** Every stage of the game gives the player a new pet moment to look forward to.
5. **Ads are wanted, not imposed.** Rewarded offers appear where the player already feels the
   problem, in the world, one at a time. Skipping never costs anything.
6. **Evidence, not claims.** A change is done when a test or a screenshot at a named viewport proves
   it, the gate is green, and a call path from normal play reaches the new code.

### 1.2 The Ice cream garden (the terrace, Batch B1)

The terrace becomes ONE simple room with its own guests. The problems it removes: guests crossing
the café-to-terrace gate back and forth (24 of 32 in one shift), an unmanned terrace register that
lost a third of its guests, guests exiting through the photo backdrop, a four-station ice cream lane
for one product with a cream supply chore and a RETURN crate nobody understood.

- **Its own entrance.** Terrace guests arrive along the west street and enter the deck through a
  garden arch in the deck's WEST edge: `terraceDoor {x:-9.6, z:12.6}`, arriving from
  `terraceSpawn {x:-11.5, z:12.6}` and leaving to `terraceSpawnOut {x:-11.5, z:12.6}`. The opening
  in the deck's west edge is z 11.6-13.6 (half-width 1.0). They leave the same way and walk off along
  the street before they are removed (fade or walk out of frame; never pop on the deck).
- **Interior guests never go outside.** They never wish for ice cream, never sit on the deck, and
  never wait beside a deck table (waitSpotFor filters by the guest's own room). gate1 is for the
  owner and staff only.
- **Terrace guests are extra guests.** Buying the garden opens a second arrival stream of its own
  (economyConfig TERRACE_DEMAND, economy.terraceSpawnInterval); it does not divert interior guests,
  whose pacing is unchanged.
- **A self-serve ice cream stand.** icecream1 (the machine) stands directly behind barIce (the stand
  counter), like the interior machine→display pairs, so the owner's work is one short shuffle. The
  machine needs no supply (cream is removed from the game). Guests queue at barIce, take a cone and
  **pay into the stand's cash jar on the spot** (a walk-past cash pile on the counter, collected
  exactly like table tips). There is no terrace register. The stand earns while the owner is inside;
  the owner's outdoor jobs are restocking cones, collecting the jar and wiping tables.
- **Tables.** Seated terrace guests pay the normal seated rate and their visit counts as a settled
  meal (Pet Book friendship). Seat choice everywhere becomes "nearest free clean seat in my room",
  so every table shows life.
- **Layout rules** (checked by tests): the gate apron |x| ≤ 2.9 for z 4.0..8.5 contains no
  footprint (nav-expanded) on either side of the fence; every station front and every queue slot
  has a clear corridor ≥ 1.0 m (the owner's body is 0.92 m wide); no spawn, exit, door or queue point
  lies inside any station's body box; after every zone build a real-movement search from where the
  build leaves the owner reaches the café centre (no pockets).
- **The interior lounge moves out of the gate apron**: tables that sat at |x| < 2.9 on the z 6.0 row
  (seat4, seat5) move elsewhere or the lounge adds two tables instead of four.

### 1.3 Photos of pets at their tables (Batch B2)

The photo booth in the deck's corner is removed: it trapped the owner, sat on the exit, detoured 40%
of paid guests across the map and needed the owner to stand there. Instead, **z_photo "Pet camera"**
unlocks photo moments everywhere:

- While photos are unlocked, at most one seated pet at a time strikes a **pose**: a camera bubble
  (the need-bubble style) pops over it. The owner walks within ~1.3 m and the existing timing-ring
  mini-game (ui/photoGame.js) plays. The shot rates, tips into that table's walk-past pile, credits
  the album, followers and the Pet Book. If nobody comes, the pose simply ends when the guest leaves.
  Pets never photographed are preferred, so photos fill the album.
- The **first** photo of each pet is the full developing-polaroid reveal; later photos are a small
  corner polaroid with no screen flash, and never while a sheet or the summary is open.
- A **photo wall** (photoWall1, wall-mounted on the west wall between the staff desk and the door, no
  floor footprint) is the physical purchase: its frames fill in as the album grows — collection
  progress you can see in the café.
- The **Photographer** is hired at the staff desk once the camera exists, spawns at the desk, and
  walks to posing pets to shoot automatically.

### 1.4 Hands that do what the player means (Batch C)

- **No accidental pickups.** A machine hands over product only when the owner has STOPPED at its
  front (speed ≈ 0), a matching display has room, and the owner did not just deliver an input in the
  same stop (fruit, beans).
- **No RETURN crates.** Items the owner no longer needs fly back to their source with a puff when
  the owner stops at a station that needs empty hands (a different machine, the pantry, a bush). No
  fee, no button, no crate. return1 and return2 are deleted.
- **No pantry sheet.** Stopping at the pantry hands over the sack the neediest connected machine
  wants (beans). A sack is used up completely by one refill. The pet treat bowl refills from its own
  kibble bin by standing at it (no sack trip across the café).
- **The smoothie corner.** The blender moves beside the fruit bushes and the smoothie counter so
  bush → blender → counter is a short triangle (≤ 3 m per hop) instead of two trips across the café.
  z_garden becomes a visible "Fruit garden" patch (soil, picket, the two bushes).
- **The kiosk goes.** Upgrades are bought from the Shop (Café button, and the staff desk), so no
  UPGRADE pill pops up while baking.
- **Guidance walks where the owner fits.** The trail is planned on a grid sized for the owner's
  body. The restock pointer never sends empty hands to an empty counter; it points at the producer.
- **Build pads** sit ≥ 1.6 m from every station front and outside every action-button radius, so a
  purchase never instantly fills the owner's hands or raises an unrelated prompt.

### 1.5 One simple menu (Batch D)

Play field: **wallet** (coins + next-build ring; tap = point me at it), **Pet Book chip**
(found/total + a dot when something is new), **Café button** (day + today's goal ring), and at most
**one offer bubble** when a rewarded offer is live. Nothing else permanent.

Behind the Café button, one page: RESUME, a sound toggle, a glyph-only Today row (phase clock, goal,
theme), and three tiles — **Pet Book**, **Shop**, **Café Stars** — plus a Daily-gift tile only when
claimable. The Shop has three tabs (Staff / Upgrades / Décor), is titled by the door that opened it,
shows only what is available now plus one teaser. Every sheet pauses the game through one modal
helper. One moment queue shows banners, toasts, discoveries and photo reveals one at a time and holds
them while any sheet is open. The day summary is: coins, chips (guests, new pets, photos), goal row,
Café Stars bar, ×2 bonus, CONTINUE.

Deleted: Franchise, Pet Socials, party orders (and the wall crate), the calendar modal, the Journey
page and its sub-pages, cafeJournal, the Boutique tab, duplicate shops, hidden HUD nodes and their
writers, dead decorators/observers, the summary's scolding tip line.

### 1.6 Reasons to keep playing (Batch E)

- **Café Stars** — one long track that replaces reputation titles, Paw Rating rows, followers,
  mastery and the weekly cup in the UI. Each star has 2-3 concrete, visible requirements and a
  visible reward (awning colour, a resident pet moves in, a décor set, legendary visitors, a café
  theme). ★5 is the Golden Paw ceremony, reachable around day 28-35 without ads.
- **Pets arrive as the café grows.** Cats and dogs from day 1; bunnies when the Pet treat bar opens;
  hamsters with the Ice cream garden; legendary pets from ★3. The Pet Book fills across the whole
  game instead of stalling at 16/20 on day 5. A new pet is a card reveal, not a toast.
- **One daily goal** (the contract and the theme day merged), shown as the ring on the Café button
  and paid at the summary.
- **After the last build**: café themes (the renovations, now bought with coins behind Café Stars,
  not reputation), seasonal décor, legendary visitors, Besties. There is always a next thing.
- **Every day has a moment**: a guaranteed new or returning named pet every 1-2 days until the book
  is complete; a short "last call" close instead of 40 s of empty café.
- **The first 3 seconds** show the café working: a guest already at the counter, a resident cat on
  its bed, a small starting balance, oven + counter + register framed in portrait.

### 1.7 Ads that earn without hurting (Batch E)

At most **3 rewarded offers per game day** plus the daily gift, **one visible at a time**, each a
world object or a single offer bubble — never buried in a menu:

1. **Day-end ×2** — "double today's earnings" (+100% of the day's sales, min 100), once per day.
2. **Special Guest** — mornings from day 3: a sparkly silhouette pet at the door; watching invites a
   pet not yet met (a guaranteed Pet Book entry); if none are left, a VIP regular (big tip, friendship).
3. **Service slot** (the first one the player meets each day):
   - **Helper Pup** — during a rush, when a counter is empty with a guest waiting or 3+ guests wait
     5 s: a pup with a crate; watching fills every counter and machine and refreshes patience.
   - **Build Boost** — standing on a pad already ≥ 40% paid (never the first pad): a ▶ badge; watching
     pays up to half the pad.
4. **Daily gift** — free to claim once per real day (welcome card after the first sale); an optional
   ▶ doubles it. A missed day pauses the streak, never resets it.
5. **Interstitial** — only at CONTINUE after completed days 3, 5, 7 …, never within 4 minutes of any
   ad and never right after a rewarded ad.

Cut: mystery gift, the old rare visitor, golden shot, rush crew, play break, roomba, smart relief,
boot interstitial, the dead speed-build wiring (replaced by Build Boost).

### 1.8 First Look — teaching new things once (Batch F)

One lane-owning system (systems/firstLook.js) shows each new thing exactly once: a short camera pan
(pan only — the camera's pitch, yaw and zoom never change) to the new station, one glyph bubble with
2-3 cells, a ghost hand only for tap/hold/timing actions, dismissed by doing it, never replayed.
Triggers: fresh save (move, bake, stock, serve), first affordable pad (build), first dirty table,
day-2 upgrades, staff desk built (hire), each role's first worker (follow it to its lane), coffee,
first dry machine (supplies), treat bar, smoothie corner, ice cream garden, pet camera (first pose).
While a lesson presents, banners and pet toasts wait. Deleted or folded: the intro's step-4
walkthrough chain, the objective's first-time walkthrough, the interaction coach's route mode, the
staff demo, and the dead hint code.

### 1.9 Looks finished (Batches B3, G)

Root causes first: 24-bit scene depth and a larger near plane (the hatching on the lawn and the
rug); the lawn as one vertex-coloured ground plane instead of 170 overlapping slabs; the deck
lowered so plank tops are y = 0 (shadows, sunk feet, the gate trench); flush floor tiles (the
crawling diagonal lines). Then: the terrace build is a reveal, not a one-frame swap; the outdoor
garden (pond, agility course, flower beds) survives the terrace by moving to a permanent pet play
yard east of the café; garden pieces are cleared by footprint, not centre point; the gate lane is
clear of planters and poles; sidewalks meet the doors; resident pets walk in along real paths; the
awning poles leave the oven and blender; décor slots never clip; blank chalkboards go (the toppers
and need bubbles already say what each station is). Bubbles for off-screen guests are not pinned to
the screen edge; name tags never drift far from their pet.

**Budget at PEAK** (whole frame, busiest state, all built): ≤ 200 draw calls, ≤ 150k triangles,
p95 frame time measured. Levers: merge static props per build group, small props and actors do not
cast shadow-map shadows (actors keep contact shadows), merge the cash trays, cap bubbles.

## 3. Save migration (all batches)

`src/sim/save.js` keeps one table of retired zones `{id, price, requires, stations}`. On load, a
retired zone that the save built (and whose prerequisite chain holds) is refunded its price, plus any
partial payment and any cash left on its stations' trays; the refund is capped, happens once (the
re-saved file never contains a retired id), and resets the ledger baseline. A carried item or sack
that no longer exists loads as empty hands. Bought accessories stay owned (and applySave must copy
`meta.accessoriesBought`, which it currently drops).
